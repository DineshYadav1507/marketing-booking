# Talent Inspirations — Terms & Conditions

**Effective date:** September 12, 2026

Talent Inspirations provides professional career-support, job-search marketing, recruitment-support, staffing and related business services. These terms are intended as a general U.S.-market commercial framework and should be reviewed by qualified U.S. counsel before publication as a final legal agreement.

## 1. Services

Services may include resume review and optimization, ATS-style readiness analysis, LinkedIn/profile positioning, job-search strategy, job-application support, recruiter outreach, interview preparation, career consulting, employer recruitment, direct hire, contract staffing, contract-to-hire and project/resource recruiting.

The exact services, deliverables, price, service period and any renewal terms are determined by the applicable order, checkout page, proposal or service agreement.

## 2. No Employment Guarantee

Talent Inspirations does **not** guarantee employment, interviews, recruiter responses, job offers, salary, compensation, placement, sponsorship, immigration approval, visa approval, work authorization, background-check approval, security clearance or continued employment.

Customers purchase professional services and support—not a job.

## 3. Candidate Responsibilities

Customers must provide accurate information and must not submit false credentials, fabricated employment history, fraudulent documents or misleading application information. Customers remain responsible for reviewing applications, resumes and other materials before submission.

## 4. Job Applications and Recruiter Outreach

Where included in a service, Talent Inspirations may help identify opportunities, organize applications, prepare application materials, submit or assist with applications, and communicate with publicly available recruiter or employer contacts. Availability, response rates and application volume vary. No outcome is guaranteed.

## 5. Resume and ATS-Style Analysis

Any resume score, keyword analysis or ATS-style assessment is an informational career-support tool. Different employers and applicant-tracking systems use different criteria. A score is not an employment decision, hiring prediction or guarantee of an interview.

## 6. AI-Assisted Services

Talent Inspirations may use software or AI-assisted tools for document analysis, keyword analysis, content organization, job matching or workflow automation. AI-generated or AI-assisted results may contain errors and should be reviewed by a person before important employment decisions or submissions.

## 7. Immigration and Legal Matters

Talent Inspirations does not provide legal advice and does not guarantee immigration, visa, sponsorship or work-authorization outcomes. Customers should consult a qualified attorney for immigration or other legal advice.

## 8. Payments

Before payment, the applicable checkout or service agreement should identify the service, price, payment frequency, service period, renewal terms (if any), cancellation terms and refund eligibility. Payments must be made only through official payment channels presented by Talent Inspirations.

## 9. Cancellation and Refunds

Refund eligibility depends on the service purchased and the stage of performance. Where legally permitted, a customer may be eligible for a refund before substantive work begins. Once customized or substantially performed professional services have begun, the amount refundable may be reduced or the completed portion may be non-refundable.

A customer is not entitled to a refund solely because an employer did not respond, an interview was not obtained, a job offer was not received, or employment was not secured. Nothing in this section limits rights that cannot lawfully be waived.

The detailed commercial policy is in `REFUND-CANCELLATION.md`.

If a service renews automatically, the renewal price, frequency and cancellation process should be disclosed before purchase. Talent Inspirations will not intentionally authorize undisclosed recurring charges.

Employer recruitment and staffing engagements may be governed by a separate client agreement covering fees, staffing rates, payment deadlines, candidate ownership, replacement terms, confidentiality, contract duration and termination.

Job opportunities may originate from third-party employers, recruiters, job boards or other sources. Talent Inspirations does not control third-party listings and cannot guarantee that a listing remains open, accurate or available.
