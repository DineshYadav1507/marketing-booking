# Talent Inspirations — Refund & Cancellation Policy

**Effective date:** September 12, 2026

## 1. What You Are Purchasing

Talent Inspirations sells professional career-support and job-search services. Payment is **not payment for a job, interview, job offer, sponsorship or guaranteed employment outcome**.

## 2. Before Work Starts

If a customer cancels before substantive work has started, the customer may request a refund. Eligibility and any applicable processing deduction will depend on the service purchased and applicable law.

## 3. After Work Starts

Once personalized work has started—such as resume editing, career analysis, application preparation, recruiter outreach, research, submissions or other customized deliverables—the refundable amount may be reduced to reflect work already performed.

Completed, delivered or substantially performed customized services may be non-refundable where permitted by law.

## 4. No-Outcome Refund

Failure to receive an interview, recruiter response, job offer, sponsorship, visa approval or employment does not by itself create a refund entitlement. Hiring decisions are controlled by employers and other third parties.

## 5. Duplicate or Unauthorized Payments

Duplicate charges or transactions that the customer genuinely believes were unauthorized should be reported promptly. Talent Inspirations will investigate and coordinate with the applicable payment processor where appropriate.

## 6. Recurring Services

For recurring services, cancellation stops future renewals according to the cancellation process disclosed at purchase. Cancellation does not automatically reverse services already provided or amounts already due.

## 7. Employer Contracts

Employer recruitment and staffing refunds, replacement guarantees, credits or fee adjustments are governed by the applicable employer agreement rather than this candidate-service policy.

## 8. How to Request Cancellation or a Refund

Send the request using the contact information provided with your order and include your name, order or booking reference, purchase date, service purchased and reason for the request. Requests are reviewed against the applicable service terms and the stage of work completed.

## 9. Legal Rights

This policy does not waive consumer rights that cannot legally be waived. If a state or federal law requires a different cancellation or refund right, that legal requirement controls.

## 10. Payment-Page Notice

> You are purchasing professional career-support and job-search services, not a job or guaranteed employment. Talent Inspirations does not guarantee interviews, recruiter responses, job offers, salary, visa sponsorship, immigration approval or placement. Please review the service description, Terms & Conditions and Refund & Cancellation Policy before payment.
