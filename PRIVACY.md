# Talent Inspirations — Privacy Notice

**Effective date:** September 12, 2026

Talent Inspirations may collect information submitted through the website, including name, email address, phone/WhatsApp number, country, visa/work-status information, target role, booking details, messages and resumes.

## How information is used

Information may be used to provide requested career-support or recruitment services, schedule and administer appointments, communicate with customers, analyze resumes, process applications when authorized, provide customer support, maintain security, prevent abuse, maintain business records and comply with legal obligations.

## Resume information

Resumes may contain sensitive professional and personal information. Access should be limited to people and service providers who need the information to perform the requested service. Resume files and extracted resume text should not be exposed through public website directories.

## Service providers

Talent Inspirations may use third-party providers for hosting, email, payments, messaging, analytics, document processing and other business operations. Their handling of information is governed by their applicable terms and privacy practices.

## Payments

Where possible, payment-card information should be handled directly by a reputable payment processor. The website should not store raw card numbers unless the business has the required security, compliance and operational controls.

## AI-assisted processing

AI-assisted software may be used for resume analysis, keyword analysis, job matching, content organization or workflow automation. Customers should not assume that an AI result is error-free or that it constitutes a hiring decision or legal advice.

## Security

Reasonable administrative, technical and organizational safeguards should be used to protect personal information. No internet-based system can guarantee absolute security.

## Retention and deletion

Information should be retained only as long as reasonably necessary for the service, legitimate business purposes, legal obligations and dispute resolution. Requests regarding personal information should be handled according to applicable law.
