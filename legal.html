About the Company

Shavya Talent Recruitment provides USA-focused talent recruitment, staffing, career support and project/resource recruiting services. Our engagements may include candidate profile positioning, job-search support, recruiter/vendor outreach, employer sourcing, screening, interview coordination, project staffing and onboarding coordination.

We are a recruitment and career-support service provider. We do not guarantee employment, interviews, a particular salary, visa approval, immigration outcome or placement. Results depend on candidate qualifications, employer requirements, market conditions and other factors outside our control.
