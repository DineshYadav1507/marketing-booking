FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Clear answers before
we get started.

Whether you are a candidate, employer or project team, here is how the engagement works.

Do you support candidates looking for USA jobs?

Yes. Candidate support can include profile positioning, ATS optimization, target-role strategy, application marketing, recruiter outreach, interview preparation and offer/joining coordination.

What kind of employer requirements can you handle?

We can structure IT, non-IT, direct hire, contract, contract-to-hire and recurring staffing requirements based on role, location, skills and hiring model.

Do you provide project or resource recruiting?

Yes. Project recruiting can cover skill-matrix planning, resource sourcing, technical screening, client review, deployment and continuity support.

Can I book a consultation before choosing a service?

Yes. Use the consultation form below and select the closest requirement. We will use the information to understand the engagement before the conversation.

Do you guarantee a job or hiring outcome?

No responsible recruiter can guarantee an offer or hire. Our role is to improve positioning, sourcing, screening, coordination and process execution while keeping expectations transparent.

How does the roadmap work?

Select Candidates, Employers or Project Recruiting. Each mode shows its own eight-stage journey. Click or hover a stage to see what we do, what happens next and the expected outcome.
