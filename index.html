WHAT HAPPENS BEHIND THE SCENES

More than an application.
A recruitment operating system.

01

Profile / Requirement Intake

We capture the information that actually drives a search: skills, seniority, location, work authorization, target role, business need, timeline and hiring model.

02

Market & Skill Mapping

We translate the requirement into a practical sourcing strategy and match candidate profiles against role fit rather than simply collecting resumes.

03

Sourcing & Marketing

Targeted applications, talent networks, recruiter/vendor outreach, hotlists and requirement-based pitching keep the right profiles visible.

04

Screening & Evaluation

We review experience, skills, communication, availability, location and work authorization before moving a profile forward.

05

Interview & Offer Coordination

We coordinate conversations, preparation, feedback, offer discussions and next steps so momentum is not lost between stages.

06

Onboarding & Follow-through

After selection, we support the transition into the engagement and maintain communication around joining or deployment.
