FAQ

Questions before
you start.

Simple answers about the platform and services.

What is Talent Inspirations?

A career and recruitment operating system connecting candidate preparation, job discovery, recruiter outreach and employer hiring workflows.

Can you help me find a USA job?

We can provide career and application support, job targeting, profile positioning, recruiter outreach and interview preparation. We do not guarantee employment, interviews or offers.

Can employers use the platform?

Yes. Employer workflows can cover requirement intake, sourcing, screening, shortlist management, interview coordination and hiring follow-through.

Is the ATS score a guarantee?

No. ATS-style readiness is an advisory indicator, not a score from a specific employer ATS and not a hiring prediction.

Does AI make hiring decisions?

No. AI can support recommendations and analysis, but people should review important candidate and hiring decisions.

Can I talk to someone before starting?

Yes. Use the consultation form below and select candidate, employer or project team.
